Where It All Started

Gofer AI: From Hackathon-Winning Project To Robotics Start-Up Company

February 2026

A Special Message

Before we start, we at the Gofer AI Team wanted to properly welcome you all to our very first article on the Gofer AI Newsletter! We're truly excited to catch you up on everything that we've been doing ever since we began working on this project in February. Thank you for coming along on this ride and we hope you enjoy learning about our company's history, mission, and future goals.

The Hackathon

Every great idea starts somewhere. For Gofer AI, our journey began during the LabLab 'Launch & Fund Your Own Startup' Hackathon, powered by Surge and hosted through LabLab.ai. This hackathon brought together innovators, developers, and entrepreneurs to explore how artificial intelligence could be applied to one of the most exciting frontiers in technology: robotics. Over the course of the challenge, teams explored emerging technologies, built prototypes, and transformed their ambitious ideas into real, working demonstrations.

The Birth of Gofer AI

The challenge began on February 6th, 2026, and ended after eight intense days of work on the 14th. We were encouraged to move beyond traditional AI applications and develop solutions that could resolve existing gaps in robotics technology with AI. The gap we chose to focus on filling was systems that use simulation as the primary environment for training, testing, or validating robotic behaviors before deployment on real robots. From that broad idea, our team brainstormed the precise solution we wanted to target: building the infrastructure needed to help robots learn directly from real-world experiences.

Our project, Gofer AI: Semantic VLA RAG Layer, transformed human demonstration videos into structured semantic memory, generated simulation data, and trained manipulation programs for real robots. By connecting human knowledge, artificial intelligence, simulation environments, and robotic systems, we aimed to create a more scalable pathway toward capable and adaptable robots.

This project was the start of what we are still diligently crafting into Gofer AI today.

Winning Second Place & Showcasing Our Demo

After developing and presenting our project during the hackathon, we are excited to share that Gofer AI won second place in the hackathon. This recognition was an incredible milestone for our team and validated the potential of our approach.

Following the competition, we had the opportunity to showcase our work through a Surge livestream, where we demonstrated our technology and shared more about the vision behind Gofer AI. The livestream allowed us to highlight our progress, explain our approach, and connect with a broader community interested in the future of AI and robotics.

Continuing the Journey

What started as a hackathon project has grown into a mission to solve one of the biggest challenges in modern robotics: the data bottleneck. By making robotic learning more efficient and accessible, Gofer AI is working toward a future where intelligent robots can better understand and interact with the real world.

We're grateful for the opportunities, communities, and collaborators that helped us take our first steps. From our first prototype at the hackathon to earning recognition through the competition, each milestone has strengthened our commitment to advancing the future of robotics.

This is only the beginning, and we can't wait to continue building the future of robotics with you.
