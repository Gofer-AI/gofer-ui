How To Win A Hackathon: Our Toolkit And How AWS Kiro Made Our Vision Possible

March 2026

AWS Kiro Interview: Accelerating Innovation Through Agentic Engineering

Welcome back to the Gofer AI Newsletter! We've got some incredibly exciting news to share!

Our Gofer AI founders, Jason and Chidi, recently sat down with Saurabh Dahal, Developer Advocate Engineer at Amazon Web Services, to talk about our big win at the LabLab Hackathon. But more than just celebrating the victory, we got to share our passionate takes on the technology that helped us successfully cross the finish line.

From AI Coding to Agentic Engineering

If you've ever competed in a hackathon, you know that time is your absolute worst enemy. For us, the ultimate key to victory against that pressure was AWS' Kiro tool. We've all used AI to help us write code, but AWS Kiro helped us jump from simple AI-assisted coding to true agentic engineering. For those unfamiliar with the term, agentic engineering differs from run-of-the-mill prompting or vibe-coding in its reliable, structured, multi-layered approach that effectively streamlines the creation of optimized products. Humans define the goals and constraints while autonomous AI agents handle the planning, writing, testing, and iteration.

Kiro instantly turned our high-level prompts into an executable, ready-to-run product. Overall, it managed to cut down our standard processing times by an impressive factor of three. Kiro also greatly cut our costs by optimizing our token usage, especially important for an early-stage start-up like ours. In a high-pressure environment where every second and dollar counts, Kiro was the glue that held our project together and helped Gofer AI take its very first steps on the path we walk today as a rising start-up.

Sharing Our Journey

A massive thank you to Saurabh Dahal and the Amazon Web Services team for helping us share the tools that got us to where we are today. We appreciate the opportunity to share how emerging AI tools like Kiro are changing the way teams like ours build, experiment, and innovate.
