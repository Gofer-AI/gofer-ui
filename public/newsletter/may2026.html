Taking the Stanford Stage, Google & NVIDIA Partnerships, & First Open Source Release

May 2026

May 2026: A Month of Milestones

May marked an exciting period of growth for Gofer AI as we reached several important milestones across both strategic partnerships and product development. From presenting our vision at Stanford University to joining leading startup programs and launching our first open-source project, each achievement represents another step toward accelerating the future of robotics and machine learning.

Stanford Innovation: Breakthrough Demo Day

Our founders Jason and Chidi had the opportunity to present Gofer AI at Stanford University's Breakthrough Demo Day. It was a day of excitement and progress for Gofer AI fueled by the energy of the campus and its brilliant minds always looking ahead. It was an incredible experience connecting with founders, investors, researchers, and students while showcasing our Gofer AI demo.

We would like to extend a special thank you to the Stanford Breakthrough Venture Accelerator Program for helping facilitate this visit and supporting emerging startups like ours throughout their growth journey.

Joining the NVIDIA Inception Program

We are proud to announce that Gofer AI has officially joined the NVIDIA Inception Program. This partnership provides our team with access to valuable technical resources, developer tools, educational opportunities, and a global community of AI startups. We are excited to continue leveraging NVIDIA's ecosystem as we push the innovation cycle for robotics forward and develop solutions that help unlock scalable robot learning.

Accepted into Google for Startups

Gofer AI has also been accepted into the Google for Startups program, providing access to Google Cloud Platform resources that will help accelerate our infrastructure and technical development. These resources allow us to continue building our core system while expanding the capabilities of our platform.

Our First Open-Source Project

Aside from the exciting new partnerships and live demos, we also hit another major milestone by open-sourcing our first project, Gofer Trace, during the AMD Developer Hackathon. Open collaboration has always been an important part of the AI and robotics community, and releasing our first public project reflects our commitment to building alongside founders, developers, and engineers around the world.

Each of these milestones reinforces our mission to remove barriers that slow the deployment of AI-augmented machine learning in robotics. By combining strategic partnerships, cutting-edge research, open-source collaboration, and continuous product development, Gofer AI is building the tools that empower the next generation of robotics innovation.

We're incredibly grateful to everyone who has supported our journey so far. From our partners and mentors to our growing community of supporters, we look forward to sharing even more exciting updates as we continue building the future of robotics together.
