Power Moves & Product Release

Taking the Stanford Stage, Google & NVIDIA Partnerships, & First Open Source Release

May 2026

A Historic Month for Gofer AI

May 2026 was one of our most action-packed months yet. Between taking the stage at one of Silicon Valley's most prestigious demo events, earning acceptance into two elite startup programs, and releasing our first open-source contribution, we barely had time to catch our breath!

Stanford Breakthrough Demo Day

In early May, Gofer AI was selected to present at the Stanford Breakthrough Demo Day — one of the most competitive startup pitch events in Silicon Valley. In front of a packed audience of investors, engineers, and industry leaders, our founder delivered a compelling pitch showcasing Gofer AI's spatial data pipelines and our vision for the future of embodied intelligence.

Google for Startups & NVIDIA Inception

We are thrilled to share that Gofer AI has been formally accepted into both the Google for Startups program and the NVIDIA Inception for Startups accelerator. These partnerships bring us:

  • Access to Google Cloud compute resources and AI/ML tooling
  • Strategic guidance from NVIDIA's deep learning and robotics expert teams
  • Networking opportunities with leading researchers and enterprise partners
  • Mentorship and advisory support from both programs' dedicated startup teams

Our First Open Source Release

Staying true to our commitment to the open robotics community, we released our first open-source contribution this month. This initial release includes core data processing utilities from our Gofer Trace pipeline, designed to make high-quality robotics dataset curation more accessible to developers and researchers around the world.

Go For It!
-The Gofer AI Team
