Coming Soon: The Gofer AI Beta for Organizations

Coming Soon

The Countdown Begins

We are thrilled to officially announce that the Gofer AI Beta is coming soon! After months of intensive engineering, software development, model fine-tuning, and collaboration with our accelerators, interns, and partners, our platform is opening its doors to partner organizations, robotics labs, and early visionaries around the globe.

Unlocking the Next Era of Robotics & Spatial AI Data

At Gofer AI, our core vision has always been centered on solving the fundamental data bottleneck in embodied intelligence. Training autonomous systems requires high-precision, real-world data pipelines that bridge computer vision, spatial reasoning, and real-time robotic action. The Gofer AI Beta will help us share that vision with the world.

Key Highlights of the Gofer AI Beta

  • Automated Data Curation & Quality Inspection: Instant ingestion and filtering of real-world visual data with built-in quality scoring and anomaly detection.
  • Real-Time Multimodal Tracking & Annotation: Advanced video-based hand tracking, object interaction modeling, and spatial trajectory extraction built for complex manipulation tasks.
  • Open & Developer-Friendly APIs: Seamless interoperability with Gofer Trace and industry-standard robotics frameworks, allowing developers to plug Gofer AI directly into existing workflows.

Early Access & Developer Onboarding

At launch, selected partner organizations and developers will receive priority access to test drive the platform, generate custom dataset runs, and collaborate directly with our engineering team to shape the roadmap for full public release.

We'd love for you to join us on this journey! If you're excited to shape the future of spatial AI and get hands-on experience with the Gofer AI Beta, reach out to our team here and claim your spot.

We at the Gofer AI team want to extend our deepest gratitude to our mentors, advisory network, summer intern cohort, and community supporters who have helped us reach this major company milestone. This is just the beginning of what we are building together, and we can't wait to show you what we have so far.

Go For It!
-The Gofer AI Team
