How To Win A Hackathon: Our Toolkit & How AWS Kiro Made Our Vision Possible

March 2026

AWS Kiro Interview: Accelerating Innovation Through Agentic Engineering

Our Gofer AI founders, Jason and Chidi, recently sat down with Saurabh Dahal, Developer Advocate Engineer at Amazon Web Services, to talk about our big win at the LabLab Hackathon.

From AI Coding to Agentic Engineering

If you've ever competed in a hackathon, you know that time is your absolute worst enemy. For us, the ultimate key to victory was AWS' Kiro tool. AWS Kiro helped us jump from simple AI-assisted coding to true agentic engineering — where humans define the goals and constraints while autonomous AI agents handle the planning, writing, testing, and iteration.

Kiro instantly turned our high-level prompts into an executable, ready-to-run product. Overall, it managed to cut down our standard processing times by an impressive factor of three. Kiro also greatly cut our costs by optimizing our token usage — especially important for an early-stage start-up like ours.

Our Toolkit

  • AWS Kiro — Agentic development environment for rapid prototyping
  • AWS Bedrock — Foundation model access for our semantic reasoning layer
  • AWS S3 & Lambda — Data storage and processing infrastructure
  • Python & PyTorch — Core ML framework for model development
  • OpenCV & MediaPipe — Computer vision for hand tracking and scene understanding

Sharing Our Journey

A massive thank you to Saurabh Dahal and the Amazon Web Services team for helping us share the tools that got us to where we are today. Check out the full interview on the Kiro YouTube channel.
