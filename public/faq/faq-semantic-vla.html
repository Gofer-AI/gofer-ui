Robotics Terminology

What is a Semantic VLA?

A Semantic Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model is a neural network architecture that integrates three modalities into a unified decision-making system:

  • Vision — the ability to perceive and interpret visual inputs (camera feeds, depth sensors, etc.)
  • Language — the ability to understand and reason with natural language instructions, descriptions, and goals
  • Action — the ability to generate precise physical actions (robot arm movements, navigation commands, etc.) based on visual and linguistic context

Why "Semantic"?

The "semantic" qualifier refers to the model's ability to understand meaning rather than just recognizing raw patterns. A semantic VLA doesn't just detect that a cup exists; it understands that the cup is on the edge of the table, that the user wants it placed in the cabinet, and what sequence of movements achieves that goal safely.
